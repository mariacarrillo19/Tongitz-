Your hand

Rules in this first version

Dealer starts with 13 cards; others get 12. Make three- or four-of-a-kind, or suited runs of at least three cards. A turn is draw → optional meld/layoff → discard. The first player to empty their hand wins. If the stock runs out, the lowest remaining hand points wins. Aces are 1, face cards 10. This is a playable simplified variant, not yet the full official Tongits rules (no draw challenge, burn, or full sapaw restrictions). Online rooms use a third-party peer connection service and are experimental.
